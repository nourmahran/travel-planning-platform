Prompt:
Design the budget summary response: totals per category, per
day, per city group and for the whole itinerary, converted
into a display currency, and explain where rounding belongs.

How the output was used:
Each source total is converted once and rounded to 2 decimals
instead of converting entry by entry, so no rounding drift
appears. Transport costs stay a separate list plus a grand
total line. Activity costs are counted under the activities
category so they are not invisible in the budget.

Prompt:
Implement the public itinerary search, the public detail and

cannot be discovered through any of them.

How the output was used:
Search matches only public, non-hidden itineraries, with the
title regex escaped. Public detail returns 404 for private or
hidden itineraries and never exposes budget totals. The clone
rebuilds every subdocument with new ids, records the original
author in clonedFrom and increments the source cloneCount.
