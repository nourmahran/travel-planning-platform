Entry 2 - 2026-11-15 - Module design
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Prompt summary:
  "Design a NestJS + MongoDB module for the assigned hotel requirements. It must prevent double
  booking under concurrency, support all-or-nothing multi-room holds that expire after 10 minutes,
  keep prices and cancellation policies stable per reservation, and avoid hard dependencies on
  other members' code."

AI output used:
  - Mongoose schemas: hotels, rooms, room_availability_blocks, room_night_locks, hotel_bookings,
    reviews.
  - Concurrency design: one lock document per room per night with a unique {roomId, date} index;
    booking + locks written in one MongoDB transaction; moderator blocks also take BLOCK locks so
    block-vs-booking races are safe.
  - Money as integer minor units; stay dates as YYYY-MM-DD calendar strings; check-out exclusive.
  - Booking state machine HELD -> CONFIRMED/CANCELLED/EXPIRED and CONFIRMED -> COMPLETED.
  - Policy snapshot (check-in/out times + cancellation tiers) stored on the reservation.
  - Ports for payments and notifications, with logging defaults for standalone use.

Human review:
  - Checked that the unique index covers exactly the sold inventory and that expired locks are
    removed before re-selling a night.
  - Confirmed the design matches the team's shared rules (pagination 10, server-side validation,
    empty lists, Swagger).
